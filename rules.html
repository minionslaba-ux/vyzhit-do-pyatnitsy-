Rules of Survive till Friday

You’re an office worker. Every card is a situation at work with two answers. Your job: make it to Friday, 6 PM without any of your four meters snapping.

1. Goal and the week

A week is 25 decisions: five a day, Monday to Friday. Cards arrive at 9:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00. Answer all 25 without dropping a meter and you’ve made it to Friday.

A week takes 5–8 minutes. In Career mode you can pause a week with “Exit” — “Play” picks up from the same card.

2. Four meters

  • 🧠 Sanityyour nerves and peace of mind
  • ⚡ Energyyour stamina
  • 👔 Bosshow management sees you
  • 🤝 Teamyour coworkers

Each meter runs from 0% to 100%. The Daily Week starts every meter at 50%. In Career mode meters carry over from the previous week, and your profession shifts the starting values — a Developer gets Sanity +15 but Team −5.

0–20% “on the edge”20–80% safe80–100% “overheat”

The week ends if any meter hits 0% or 100%. Too good is bad too: a Boss at 100% buries you in work, a Team at 100% won’t let you work. Keep every meter between 20% and 80%.

3. How to answer

  • Drag the card left or right, tap the answer buttons, or use the ← → keys.
  • While you drag, ▲ and ▼ light up over the meters — what goes up and what goes down. ▲▲ or ▼▼ means a big swing.
  • A card with a ← arrow is the fallout of an earlier choice. Coworkers remember: a friend will bail you out, someone you burned will get even.
  • Some weeks are special: quarterly reports (every 13th career week), the week before vacation, your first week at a new job with a new Boss. In some weeks the Boss’s mood is hidden — you see “?” instead of arrows.

4. Items and second chance

You start a week with 1–2 items. Each one automatically blocks one hit of −10 or worse to its meter. A ◆ over a meter means the next hit will be absorbed.

Meter hit the edge? Once per week you get a second chance: a trivia question with 20 seconds on the clock. Answer right and the week goes on.

5. Pay, expenses, shop

In Career mode every workday pays a salary of $3, $4 or $5 depending on the company — a bank text tells you when it lands. At the end of the week rent and food take two days’ pay, plus a loan payment if you have one.

Below zero means debt: every Monday Sanity −10 until you’re back in the black.

Open the shop with the “$ · shop” button under the clock during a week:

6. Deadlines

On career mornings a 📋 task with a deadline may show up. Take it or pass. If you take it, make enough work decisions before it’s due — answers that please the Boss or cost Energy. They’re marked “📋 to the task”, and the deadline bar shows your progress.

Miss the deadline and the penalty comes off your balance.

7. The 52-week career

Career mode is a year of 52 weeks. Pick a profession and a personality trait, get hired, and live it week by week. Money and progress carry over.

  • Fired (a meter hit 0% or 100%)? Time to job hunt: pick an opening and pass a 7-question interview. Tougher companies pay more.
  • Vacation: you earn 28 days a year, about half a day per week. Save up 7 days to take a week off with vacation pay.
  • Some stories start one week and pay off the next.

Your character

At the start of a career you pick a 3D character — one of ten (five men and five women, some married, some single) — and give them a name and a job. Their needs grow over time: proper food, utilities, a coworker’s birthday gift, money for auntie, a better apartment, a phone, a car, a vacation, a wedding, a down payment on a home. After each work week they ask for something: pay for it, buy on credit (repay 20% more), take a bonus task (a deadline is guaranteed next week) or put it off — and they get upset. An unhappy character costs Sanity −10 at the start of the week; a happy one gives +5. Once a week you can send them on a $20 course for +60 XP.

9. Daily Week, quests, challenges

  • The Daily Week is the same for every player that day. Play it and compare on the “Day” leaderboard; after each card you see how many players chose the same answer.
  • Daily quests: three goals every day. Each one gives +30 XP and +$4; finish all three for +1 item next week and +50 XP.
  • Challenge: send a coworker a link to your week — they play the exact same one and try to beat you.
  • Departments compete on their average score for the calendar week, and there are office tournaments with a results screen.

10. Office, chats, sign-in

You can play as a guest, no sign-up needed. An account unlocks your own Office, the office chat, the general chat, direct messages, suggesting your own cards, and shared progress across phone and computer.

Sign in with email (an email link or a password), with Telegram or with VK ID. After you log out, the game asks you to sign in again.

Everything about your account lives under the “My profile” badge in the top-right corner: name and department, password, Telegram and VK links, language, log out. You can also delete your account there: confirm it via the link in an email (or by signing in again for Telegram/VK accounts), and the account and all its data are erased for good.

Characters marked “game character 🎭” aren’t real people — they live in the game’s office.
