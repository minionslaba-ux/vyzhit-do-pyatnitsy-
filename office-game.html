Office game online: “Survive till Friday”

Monday, 9 a.m. Your boss texts “got a minute?”, the team chat is fighting over the AC, and a client sent a brief in ALL CAPS. Your job: make it to Friday, 6 p.m., with your sanity intact. Free, in your browser, no sign-up.

Play now Game rules

How to play

Each week is 25 cards: five a day, morning to evening, Monday through Friday. Swipe a card left or right to decide. Every decision moves four meters:

  • Sanity — your peace of mind
  • Energy — how much you have left in the tank
  • Boss — how happy management is with you
  • Team — how you get along with coworkers

Zero ends the week. But so does 100%: becoming the boss’s pet or the life of every party overheats just the same. Keep the balance.

Modes

  • A 52-week career: salary, 28 vacation days a year, getting fired, job hunting and interviews.
  • Daily Week: the same week for every player. Compare scores with coworkers and challenge them with a link.
  • Department leaderboard: Marketing vs Accounting — whose department lasts longer?
  • Office tournament: a shared start, one week for everyone and a results screen for the projector.

For teams and HR

Create your own office, invite coworkers with a link and chat in the office chat. For onboarding there are branded weeks: cards about company rules with a “how we do it here” tip after each answer.

FAQ

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can play as a guest. An email sign-up is only needed for your own office, chats and moving your progress to another device.

How long is one game?

A week is 25 decisions, about 5–8 minutes. A career lasts 52 weeks.

Can I play with my coworkers?

Yes. The Daily Week is the same for every player, and there are department leaderboards, offices with a chat and tournaments with a results screen for the projector.

Is the game free?

Yes, the game is free and runs in the browser on phones and computers. You can add it to your home screen or open it in Telegram.
